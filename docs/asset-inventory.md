Asset Inventory

1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to identify and classify important assets belonging to GreenCare Community Hospital.

Understanding what needs to be protected is an important first step in cybersecurity risk management.

---

2. Asset Inventory

| Asset ID | Asset | Description | Importance |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-01 | Patient Database | Stores sensitive patient records and medical information | Critical |
| A-02 | Staff Computers | Computers used by employees for daily operations | High |
| A-03 | Email System | Used for internal and external communication | High |
| A-04 | Hospital Wi-Fi | Provides wireless network access to staff devices | High |
| A-05 | Public Website | Provides information about the hospital and its services | Medium |
| A-06 | File-Sharing System | Stores and shares internal organizational documents | High |
| A-07 | Network Infrastructure | Includes routers, switches, and other networking equipment | High |

---

3. Asset Classification

Assets were classified according to their potential impact on the organization if compromised, unavailable, modified, or destroyed.

Critical

Assets containing highly sensitive information or supporting essential operations.

Example: Patient Database.

High

Assets that support important organizational activities or provide access to sensitive systems.

Examples: Staff Computers, Email System, Hospital Wi-Fi, File-Sharing System, and Network Infrastructure.

Medium

Assets whose compromise could affect the organization but would have a lower overall impact than critical systems.

Example: Public Website.

---

4. Key Observation

The Patient Database represents the most critical asset because unauthorized access, modification, destruction, or unavailability could have serious consequences for patients and hospital operations.

Protecting critical assets should therefore receive the highest priority.
