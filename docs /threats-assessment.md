1. Purpose

This document identifies realistic cybersecurity threats that could affect GreenCare Community Hospital.

The assessment focuses on threats that could compromise the organization's systems, information, or operations.

---

2. Identified Threats

| Threat ID | Threat | Description | Potential Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| T-01 | Phishing | Attackers attempt to trick employees into revealing credentials or opening malicious content | Account compromise, malware infection |
| T-02 | Malware | Malicious software infects organizational systems | Data loss, system disruption |
| T-03 | Ransomware | Attackers encrypt systems or data and demand payment | Operational disruption, data unavailability |
| T-04 | Credential Compromise | User credentials are stolen or exposed | Unauthorized access |
| T-05 | Insider Threat | An employee intentionally or accidentally misuses access | Data exposure, modification, or loss |

---

3. Threat Analysis

T-01 — Phishing

Employees may receive convincing fraudulent emails designed to steal usernames and passwords or deliver malicious files.

Potential targets:

- Email accounts
- Staff Computers
- Internal Systems

Potential impact:

- Credential theft
- Account compromise
- Malware infection
- Unauthorized access

---

T-02 — Malware

Malware could enter the environment through malicious downloads, email attachments, compromised websites, or removable media.

Potential targets:

- Staff Computers
- File-Sharing System
- Network Resources

Potential impact:

- Data loss
- System compromise
- Operational disruption

---

T-03 — Ransomware

Ransomware could disrupt hospital operations by encrypting important files and systems.

Potential targets:

- Patient Database
- File-Sharing System
- Staff Computers

Potential impact:

- Loss of data availability
- Operational disruption
- Financial losses
- Recovery costs

---

T-04 — Credential Compromise

Weak passwords, password reuse, phishing, or credential theft could allow attackers to gain access to internal systems.

Potential targets:

- Email System
- User Accounts
- Patient Systems
- Administrative Systems

Potential impact:

- Unauthorized access
- Data exposure
- Account takeover

---

T-05 — Insider Threat

An authorized employee could intentionally or accidentally expose, modify, or misuse sensitive information.

Potential targets:

- Patient Database
- File-Sharing System
- Email System
- Staff Computers

Potential impact:

- Data exposure
- Data modification
- Data loss
- Privacy violations

---

4. Threat-to-Asset Mapping

| Threat | Primary Target | Potential Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Phishing | Email System / Staff Computers | Credential theft and malware infection |
| Malware | Staff Computers / File-Sharing System | System compromise and data loss |
| Ransomware | Patient Database / File-Sharing System | Data unavailability and operational disruption |
| Credential Compromise | Email System / User Accounts | Unauthorized access |
| Insider Threat | Patient Database / File-Sharing System | Data exposure or unauthorized modification |

---

5. Key Observation

Several threats can exploit the same underlying weaknesses.

For example:

Phishing → Stolen Credentials → Unauthorized Account Access → Sensitive Data Exposure

This demonstrates why cybersecurity risk assessment should examine the relationship between threats and vulnerabilities rather than treating them as isolated problems.
